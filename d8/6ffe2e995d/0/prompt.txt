This job requests security-events: write and runs on pull_request. For PRs opened from forks, the GITHUB_TOKEN typically does not have security-events: write, so the CodeQL analyze step’s SARIF upload can fail and turn the check red for external contributors. Consider gating the upload (or the whole job) to same-repo PRs (e.g., if: github.event.pull_request.head.repo.full_name == github.repository) or conditionally disabling upload when running from a fork.
