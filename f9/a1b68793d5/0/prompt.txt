The post-prompt polling loop uses idleDeadline := time.Now().Add(timeout) after WaitFor(..., timeout) has already potentially consumed the full timeout, so the overall RunPrompt call can block for up to ~2× timeout (and it doesn’t honor early ctx cancellation inside the loop). Consider tracking a single absolute deadline for the whole operation and using the remaining time for the "ctrl+c to stop" wait, and exit early when ctx.Done() is closed.
